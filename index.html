What is Autism?

Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) is a developmental disorder that affects communication, behavior, and social interaction. It can vary widely in symptoms and severity from person to person.

Terms You Should Know:

  • Jaundice: A yellowing of the skin and eyes that sometimes occurs in newborns. It is usually harmless and goes away with time or treatment.
  • Screening Test: A medical or psychological test to detect potential health or developmental issues early.

Why It’s Important to Check for ASD Even in Adulthood

1. Symptoms Might Be Subtle or Missed in Childhood: Many adults with ASD were never diagnosed as children because their symptoms were mild or not recognized. Adult diagnosis helps identify these cases.

2. Better Understanding of Yourself: Getting a diagnosis can help individuals understand their thinking patterns, behaviors, and challenges, improving self-awareness and self-management.

3. Access to Appropriate Support: Diagnosis opens doors to therapies, resources, and accommodations that can improve quality of life in work, social situations, and mental health.

4. Improved Social Interaction: Knowing about ASD can help both the individual and those around them communicate and relate more effectively.

5. Reducing Psychological Burden: Many adults with undiagnosed ASD face anxiety or depression without knowing why. Diagnosis can be the first step to appropriate treatment and relief.
